# Product catalogue

**Fictional demo data.** Everything below is invented for a proof-of-concept.
It describes no real operator, tariff or price. Edit freely — the assistant
treats this file as its only source of product facts, so whatever is here is
what it will quote. `{brand}` is replaced with the configured brand name.

Currency is GBP. Every plan is SIM-only (no handset) and includes unlimited
calls and texts to standard domestic numbers.

## 30-day rolling SIM-only plans

| Plan | Data | Price / month | International minutes | Roaming data included |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lite | 5 GB | £6 | 100 mins to 30 destinations | 5 GB in Zone A |
| Everyday | 15 GB | £9 | 300 mins to 50 destinations | 10 GB in Zone A |
| Plus | 30 GB | £12 | 500 mins to 50 destinations | 15 GB in Zone A |
| Max | 100 GB | £18 | 1000 mins to 60 destinations | 25 GB in Zone A |
| Unlimited | Unlimited | £25 | Unlimited to 60 destinations | 30 GB in Zone A |

No credit check, no exit fee, cancel or switch plan any time before your
renewal date.

## 12-month SIM-only plans

Same allowances as above, £2/month cheaper on every tier — so Lite is £4,
Everyday £7, Plus £10, Max £16, Unlimited £23. Price is fixed for the full
12 months. Leaving early costs the remainder of the term.

## Data rollover

Unused data rolls over for one month on Plus, Max and Unlimited. Lite and
Everyday do not roll over. Rolled-over data is used first, before the new
month's allowance.

## Add-ons

| Add-on | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Data Booster 5 GB | £3 | Valid until your next renewal date |
| Data Booster 20 GB | £8 | Valid until your next renewal date |
| International 500 | £5 / month | 500 extra international minutes |
| Roaming Pass Zone B | £6 / day | 2 GB per day outside Zone A |
| Voicemail Plus | Free | Transcribed voicemail to text |

## Roaming

- **Zone A** — 45 destinations across Europe. Included up to the plan's roaming
  allowance, then £2 per GB.
- **Zone B** — 60 destinations including the US, Canada, Australia and much of
  Asia. Not included on any plan; needs a Roaming Pass at £6/day.
- **Zone C** — everywhere else. Pay as you go at £6 per GB, calls £1.50/min.

Roaming is off by default on new accounts and can be switched on in the
{brand} app under Settings, Roaming.

## Top-ups and payment

Plans are billed monthly by card or Direct Debit. Pay-as-you-go credit can be
added by card in the {brand} app, in your online account, by auto top-up, or
with a voucher bought from a shop. Credit is used to renew your bundle on your
renewal date, so it needs to be there before then. Card top-ups can take a few
minutes to show.

## Switching to {brand}

Keeping your number takes one working day: text PAC to 65075 from your current
phone, give the code to {brand} when you order, and the number transfers.
Any remaining credit or allowance on your old plan does not transfer. A PAC
code is valid for 30 days.

## Coverage and network

5G is included at no extra cost on every plan, in 5G coverage areas, with a
5G-capable phone. 4G elsewhere. Tethering (using the phone as a hotspot) is
allowed on all plans and comes out of the plan's data allowance.

## eSIM

Supported on all plans at no extra cost. An eSIM can be issued instantly from
the {brand} app; one swap per month is free, after that it is £5.
